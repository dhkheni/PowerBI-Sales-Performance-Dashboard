# 📊 PowerBI-Sales-Performance-Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project demonstrates **Data Modelling in Power BI** using a retail sales dataset. It covers relationships, cardinality, Star/Snowflake Schema, DAX measures, and interactive visualizations.

## 🗂️ Tables

* Customer
* Category
* Product
* Store
* Sales — Fact Table

## 🧮 DAX Measures

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Quantity

## 📈 Dashboard

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** includes:

* KPI Cards
* Sales Trend
* Sales by Product
* Sales by Category
* Sales & Profit by Region
* Profit by Store
* Product Performance Matrix

## 🎛️ Slicers

Year | Quarter | Region | Category | Product

## 🛠️ Tools Used

**Microsoft Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Excel**
